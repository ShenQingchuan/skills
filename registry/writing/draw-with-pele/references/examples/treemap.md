# Treemap 示例

以下来自 Pele 官方 Examples 首页的精选示例。完整语法与边界情况见 <https://pele.run/examples/treemap>。

```mermaid
---
config:
  treemap:
    valueFormat: '$0,0'
---
treemap-beta
"Budget"
    "Operations"
        "Salaries": 700000
        "Equipment": 200000
        "Supplies": 100000
    "Marketing"
        "Advertising": 400000
        "Events": 100000
```
