# Mindmap 示例

以下来自 Pele 官方 Examples 首页的精选示例。完整语法与边界情况见 <https://pele.run/examples/mindmap>。

```mermaid
mindmap
  root((Hawaiʻi))
    History
      Old legends
      ::icon(fa fa-book)
      Voyaging
        Polynesian navigators who sailed by the stars
    Nature
      Mountains<br/>and volcanoes
      Ocean life
        Reef
            Sea turtles
            Monk seals
            Parrotfish
    Culture
      Hula and lei
      ʻUkulele
```

```mermaid
mindmap
    id1["`**Pele** is the
goddess of volcanoes
Unicode works too: 🌋`"]
      id2["`She lives in **the** crater of Kīlauea... a *very old story* that is still told today`"]
      id3[Lava builds new land]
```
